Geometry laboratory

Investigation 01 / Triangle angle sum

Change two interior angles. The third angle is determined by the angle-sum property of a triangle.

The third angle is 60°.

Investigation 02 / Quadrilateral angle sum

The interior angles of a quadrilateral add up to 360°.

The fourth angle is 90°.

Investigation 03 / Pythagorean theorem

For a right-angled triangle, c² = a² + b². Explore how the hypotenuse changes with the two shorter sides.

Hypotenuse = 5 units.

Investigation 04 / Triangle measurements

Find a triangle's perimeter and area. Compare the base-height method with Heron's formula when all three sides are known.

Area by base-height = 20 square units. Heron's formula uses the three side lengths.
Use measurements from the same triangle if you want to compare both area methods. The base-height method uses the first two fields; Heron's formula uses the three side lengths.

Investigation 05 / Quadrilateral area and perimeter

Select a shape and change its dimensions. The experiment calculates area and perimeter, with a short explanation.

Square: area = side²; perimeter = 4 × side.
For a rhombus, use the side length and perpendicular height for area. For a trapezium, a and b are the parallel sides; height is perpendicular distance between them. Perimeter uses all four side lengths.
